# Interview demonstration

The revised project demonstrates a deliberately simple content-discovery journey: a question, one clearly labeled personal account, an evidence-grounded explanation, and related stories.

1. Open the demo. It begins with a welcome message and a single input.
2. Choose “Feeling loved.” Read the featured excerpt and the short explanation.
3. Use the right control under the response to view a similar story, when available. Suggestions vary from one to five accounts.
4. Type a second prompt about purpose, then use “Jump to a prompt” to review the earlier response.
5. If asked about engineering, explain the reviewed source corpus, local text retrieval, explicit theme matching, and reproducible rebuild.

The 60 excerpts are individually reviewed for accurate explicit/thematic classification and non-graphic workplace-appropriate wording. They are excerpts, not full stories, and some are short reflections. This screening is not an official content rating or a certification of the external full accounts.

The strongest product decision in this revision is making the experience easy to understand in an interview while retaining an inspectable matching pipeline behind it. The work is AI-assisted; understand the code and source limitations before presenting it as portfolio work.

Next research step: independent reviewers assess topic fit and explanation faithfulness for varied paraphrased prompts. No claim of improved user outcomes or superiority over keyword retrieval is made.
