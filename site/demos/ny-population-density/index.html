How to read this map

Each tower represents one hexagon. Color shows the density range, and tower heights use a logarithmic scale so differences outside New York City stay visible. Hover or tap a cell for its estimated density and population.

The grid uses the November 2023 Kontur release. Each hexagon is assigned to New York by its center. Density uses the entire cell area, including any water, and the base fills areas without a plotted cell.

Sources: Kontur Population (CC BY) and U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries.
