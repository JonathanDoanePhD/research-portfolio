    How to read this map

    Each tower represents one hexagon. Color shows the density range, and height is directly proportional to density. Move Granularity to the right to group cells into larger hexagons. Hover or tap a cell for its estimated density and population.

    The grid uses the November 2023 Kontur release. Original hexagons are assigned to New York by their centers. Coarser views sum their population into larger H3 cells. Density divides that sum by the full area of the larger hexagon, including any water. Large cells along the border can extend beyond New York.

    Use Geographic features to show waterways and regional boundaries, and Elevation to compare shaded terrain with the density towers. Elevation and density can show spatial patterns together, such as sparse settlement in the higher Adirondacks.

    Sources: Kontur Population (CC BY) and U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries. Elevation is from USGS 3DEP, sampled at approximately 1 km. See the geographic data sources.
