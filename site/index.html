How I work / 02

Curiosity, with a method.

Good analysis starts with the decision at stake. I work from the question through the evidence, including the uncomfortable findings and limits that determine whether a result can be trusted.

01

Frame the question

Define the outcome, competing explanations, useful baselines, and the decisions the analysis may influence.

02

Investigate and model

Clean and examine the data, engineer relevant features, compare methods, and evaluate errors.

03

Interrogate the result

Check limitations, potential bias, and whether a model's apparent gains hold up against practical alternatives.

04

Make findings useful

Explain the evidence and tradeoffs clearly to technical and nontechnical collaborators.
